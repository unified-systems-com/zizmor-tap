{% comment %} One finding, read top-down: what it is, where, the evidence, what it can reach, what to do. Two rules this template follows and its predecessor did not. A section renders ONLY when it has content. The panel computes presence (`job_facts`, `fragment`, `fixes`); nothing here prints a heading and then discovers the body is empty, because a heading over a blank body reads as "none" when it means "not recorded". A value appears ONCE. The narrowed fragment is called out only when it is genuinely narrower than the excerpt; the job's display name only when it differs from its key; the two timestamps collapse to a single line when they are the same instant. Explanations live in `title` attributes rather than paragraphs. The reasoning still needs to be reachable — this page argues about absence and provenance — but a reader who already knows should not have to scroll past the lesson every time. {% endcomment %}
